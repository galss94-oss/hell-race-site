Hell Race – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Hell Race is a racing game made by Gal Samuchyan for friends to test. This page explains what information the game handles.

What the game collects

Hell Race has no accounts, no ads, no in-app purchases and no tracking. Races against bots happen entirely on your phone, and your progress and settings stay there.

Online play with friends

When you play online, the game connects to the Hell Race game server (hosted on Oracle Cloud in Israel) and sends it:

  • the nickname you type in Settings, which the other players in your room see;
  • a random number created when the game is first installed, so the server can put you back in your seat if your connection drops (it is not linked to your name, phone number or advertising ID);
  • your race controls (steering and buttons) and the room code you join.

The server needs your phone's internet address to send the race back to it, and keeps it only while your room is open. Its log records room events with nicknames (for example "Tom joined room 4821"), is used only to fix problems, is never shared or sold, and rolls over automatically as it fills. Game traffic is not encrypted, so don't use your real name as your nickname if you'd rather not.

When it starts, the game also reads a small settings file from this website (which game server to use). That request carries nothing about you; like any web page, GitHub, which hosts this site, sees your phone's internet address.

Scoreboard

When an online match finishes, the server saves a record of it for the scoreboard: the time, and for each player the nickname, the random number above, whether they won, and their rounds won, knock-offs, falls and times eaten. The scoreboard in the game shows you your totals and those of the people you have raced; nobody else sees them. The records stay on the game server until you ask for them to be removed, and are never shared or sold.

To have your scoreboard records or log entries with your nickname removed, email the address below with your nickname.

Game engine statistics

The game is built with the Unity engine. Unity automatically sends anonymous technical information about the device, such as the phone model, operating system version, graphics chip and memory size, to Unity Technologies so that it can improve its engine. This information does not identify you. See Unity's privacy policy for players.

Test builds

When you test through Apple TestFlight or Google Play, those services may share crash reports and the feedback you choose to send with the developer, under Apple's and Google's privacy policies.

Children

Hell Race is not directed at children under 13.
