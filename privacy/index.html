Hell Race – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Hell Race is a racing game made by Gal Samuchyan for friends to test. This page explains what information the game handles.

What the game collects

Hell Race does not ask for, collect, or store any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no in-app purchases and no tracking. Your race progress and settings stay on your phone.

Game engine statistics

The game is built with the Unity engine. Unity automatically sends anonymous technical information about the device, such as the phone model, operating system version, graphics chip and memory size, to Unity Technologies so that it can improve its engine. This information does not identify you. See Unity's privacy policy for players.

Test builds

When you test through Apple TestFlight or Google Play, those services may share crash reports and the feedback you choose to send with the developer, under Apple's and Google's privacy policies.

Children

Hell Race is not directed at children under 13.

Changes and contact

If a future version adds online play or anything else that handles personal information, this page will be updated first. Questions: galss94@gmail.com.
